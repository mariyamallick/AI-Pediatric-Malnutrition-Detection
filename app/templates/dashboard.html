Understanding Growth Indicators

Indicator Description Normal Range
WAZ Weight-for-Age compares the child's weight with children of the same age. Z-score ≥ -2
HAZ Height-for-Age measures long-term growth and helps identify stunting. Z-score ≥ -2
WHZ Weight-for-Height detects wasting and acute malnutrition. Z-score ≥ -2
BMI Body Mass Index calculated from weight and height. WHO BMI-for-age Standard
MUAC Mid-Upper Arm Circumference is used for screening acute malnutrition. ≥ 12.5 cm

WHO Z-Score Interpretation

Z-Score Interpretation
≥ -2 Normal Growth
-3 to -2 Moderate Malnutrition
< -3 Severe Malnutrition
WHO Recommendation: Children with Z-scores below -2 require nutritional assessment and monitoring. Children below -3 require urgent clinical attention and nutritional intervention.
